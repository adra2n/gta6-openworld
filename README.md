**Sunburn Bay** — an original sun-soaked open-world crime game that runs in the browser from static files. A procedural seaside city of art-deco hotels, shops, towers, a marina and a working dock, explored on foot and by car: traffic that follows its lanes and stops for you, pedestrians who cross at the corners and run when shots are fired, a five-star wanted level with cruisers that chase and officers who shoot, a seven-mission story arc with chases, a timed delivery, a shootout and a heist finale, a HUD with a minimap drawn from the city's own roads, a start screen, saves, and procedural WebAudio for the engines, tyres, guns and sirens. Weather, wildlife, stunt jumps, street races, a radio dial, a police scanner, a shop economy and a journal sit on top of that. This page is the short manual: how to serve it, the keys, and what the screenshots are.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080>. Keyboard and mouse: **WASD** to move, **Shift** to sprint, **Space** to jump, **F** to enter or leave a car, **right mouse** to aim, **left mouse** to fire, **R** to reload, **Esc** for the pause menu.

Screenshots in `screenshots/` are renders of the game itself, staged by `?shot=cover|chase|mission|dusk&seed=1`.
